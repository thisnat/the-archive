Tell me about the sweetest candy,  
tell me about the lie!  
Each time Pinocchio’s timber stretches,  
the pillar of heaven drives deeper, unyielding and strong.  
When a saint paints the shadow dwelling behind the blazing sun,  
a white castle instantly appears.  
Before its gates rolls an endless tide of green,  
all of the honey poured out for only one  
on the marble cliffs, facing the sea.
