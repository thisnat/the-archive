date:   2026-8-22

It is warm, is it not?
Running to slip the grasp of a black sky,
but the moon stands at the doorway.
Oh pretty! I’m trying to dance in the light,
stay longer before the night’s blade.
A jeweled dagger in the chest, crimson flow,
the haunting of a vengeful ghost.
Wake up with the beauty, dressed by the stars.
