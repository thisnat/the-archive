Why does everyone always want to find something? They try to explain everything, trying to force themselves into other people's dreams or ideas. Just like: 'I’m an INFP, so that’s why I don’t want to go outside.' It’s always like that—that attitude of 'I’m a robot, and I’ve discovered my mechanics.'

For me, in this chaotic world—a world where angels intervene in our fate, sending a divine wind to our boat just as it nears the shore, or planting strange dust in the dirt to make our crops mutate—it is even more intense for those who live in someone else's dream. Their fate and spirit are simply taken by psychopaths. In this messy world, how do people really think they can reach a final answer? The absolute: 'Yeah, I’m sure, I’m absolutely an INFP—look, I have empathy!' It has so many layers: morality, social constructs, language, narrative, and so much more. Your MBTI is just a product of that—an autonomous, self-improving, auto-sophisticating runaway machine. I mean, it's a mess, pure chaos!

You cannot separate a single thing and consider it in isolation; everything is connected. Do one thing, and some terrifying, unknown consequence unfolds. There is a relationship between the 'changing world order' and 'how a cupcake is made'—the two are connected in ways beyond our comprehension. But remember, everything we think we know right now has no metaphysical anchor; it all floats on the void.

Do you understand what I'm saying? It means doing one small thing can dramatically alter another: you draw a strange symbol, and a restaurant you know suddenly goes out of business. Everything always holds a connection to something beyond our imagination.

So why do you limit yourself to something like MBTI or other people's dreams when we are running on the infinite with no floor, and we can still run? But I don’t mean all of you; many of them need to be trapped in a dream to maintain order, if you think order is important. I don’t know. I only know that running in someone else's dream can make you suffer, because you were damned from the start when you entered the dream, and you just cannot help yourself—yet all of you still look happy on the outside. And my only advice is: DON’T LIVE IN OTHER PEOPLE'S DREAMS.
