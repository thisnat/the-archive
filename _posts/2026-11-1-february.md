date:   2026-11-1

After the tree reaches its height,  
when dewdrops roam around apples,  
the sky forgets the shade it used to wear.  
The scent of the rain no longer carries green;  
the winter breeze has lost its blush of pink.  
Orange sunlight and its abdication—  
everything seems pale under fluorescent.  
I defy this light with the scarlet February.
